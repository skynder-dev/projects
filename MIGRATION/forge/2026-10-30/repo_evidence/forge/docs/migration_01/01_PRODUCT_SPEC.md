# PRODUCT SPEC

## What Forge is
Merch Forge is a local-first Windows workbench for discovering merch opportunities, turning research into concept candidates, reviewing concepts, preparing artwork/generation instructions, accepting exact artwork, and preparing publication-ready product packages.

## Product outcome
The user should be able to move from market/niche evidence to a small set of credible merch products without repeatedly leaving the app or manually reconstructing context.

## Core workflow target
Discover/Research -> concept draft -> concept review -> artwork/generation preparation -> exact artwork import/generation result -> visual acceptance -> metadata/policy/IP checks -> publication draft/package.

## Owner role
Owner involvement should be concentrated at genuinely subjective or irreversible gates: final creative selection/acceptance, paid-provider authorization, and external publication/submission. Routine research, drafting, validation, repair, and packaging should be autonomous.

## Non-goals
- Do not build a generic text editor.
- Do not keep adding decorative UI without completing the workflow.
- Do not fake provider results, policy clearance, IP clearance, native runtime proof, or sales validation.
- Do not optimize the product around GoDog, Supabase recovery machinery, or ChatGPT-specific execution loops.
