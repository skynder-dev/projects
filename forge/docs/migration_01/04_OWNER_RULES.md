# OWNER RULES

- Do not ask the owner to press GO or approve reversible engineering decisions.
- Do not infer creative acceptance from silence or from a technical PASS.
- Do not spend money or issue paid image-provider requests without explicit authorization.
- Do not submit/publish products externally without explicit authorization.
- Do not expose or copy secrets into source, logs, bundles, prompts, or reports.
- Keep the existing dark/orange visual direction unless a concrete usability defect requires change.
- Avoid gratuitous language-selector/UI redesign. Functional flow has priority over cosmetics.
- Do not build around owner biography, unrelated personal data, or private contacts.
- When owner input is eventually required, present one compact review packet rather than many interruptions.
