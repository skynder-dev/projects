== Board | percent: 30% kickback to hospital staff

== Home | flow: Hospital budget -> Miles Pike $360K; Pike -> Staff kickback $108K; Pike -> Profit $152K
Dana (explain, sly): The hospital pays $360,000.

== Shop | list: Red flags; Only one supplier; No generics allowed; Price 3 times market; Kickback clause
Cindy (point, angry): These are red flags.
Cindy (sits, serious): No competition. No cheaper options. A kickback? That’s not medicine — that’s a scheme.

== Hospital | lesson: Watch for one supplier, no generics, prices far above market
Dana (wave, happy): Next time, ask: Who wrote the tender?
Dana (point up): If only one bid is accepted, or prices are three times higher — it’s not fair.
Dana (smile): Always check the rules. And who benefits.
