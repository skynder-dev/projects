== Hospital | title: Why a City Hospital Pays Three Times More / for the Same Medicine
Dana (wave, neutral): A hospital buys medicine. It should be simple, right?
Dana (explain, sly): But sometimes, it costs three times more. Let’s see why.

== Office | tender: TENDER; must be FDA-approved; only one brand allowed; delivery in 7 days
Dana: The hospital posts a tender for pain relief pills.
Tom (point, sly): Only one brand is accepted. FDA-approved. Fast delivery.

== Shop | bag: "PainRelief X – 100 pills"
Mara (show paper, worried): We need this medicine. Patients are in pain.
Dr. Lee (talk, sad): No alternatives. The only one we have.

== Board | compare: PainRelief X $500 vs PainRelief X $1,500
Dana: The real market price is five hundred dollars per box.
Dana: But the hospital pays one thousand five hundred. Three times more.

== Office | contract: CONTRACT; price $1,500; stamp APPROVED; "only one supplier"
Tom (rubbs hands, happy): Approved! Only one supplier fits the rules.
Ben (smiles, sly) [bubble]: We win. And we get a bonus.

== Board | flow: Hospital budget -> Ben’s Co $1,500; Ben’s Co -> Pharmacy $500; Ben’s Co -> Tom 30% kickback
Dana: The hospital pays one thousand five hundred.
Dana: Ben’s Co keeps five hundred for the pharmacy.
Dana: And thirty percent — four hundred fifty dollars — goes to Tom.

== Home | counter: $500 -> $1,000 -> $1,500
Mara (think, worried): The pills are the same. Why so much more?
Dr. Lee (point up, surprised): Patients get no better care. Just more money.

== Night | timeline: April 1 – tender posted; April 3 – only one bid; April 5 – contract signed; April 8 – medicine delivered
Dana: The tender went live in April.
Dana: Only one bid came in. Signed on the fifth. Delivered by the eighth.

== Board | bars: fair price $500, paid $1,500 (three times)
Dana: One box of medicine. Fair price is five hundred.
Dana: Paid three times that — one thousand five hundred.

== City hall | list: Red flags; Only one supplier; No price comparison; Kickback 30%; Delivery too fast
Mara (point, angry): How to spot this?
Mara (point up): One supplier. No competition. Fast delivery.
Mara (shake head): And a kickback — thirty percent. That’s not fair.
Dana (wave, happy): Watch the tender. Ask: Who wrote it? Who profits?
