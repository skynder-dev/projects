== Hospital | title: Why a City Hospital Pays Three Times More / for the Same Medicine
Dana (wave, neutral): A hospital buys medicine. Should cost about $200 per bottle.
Dana (explain, sly): But sometimes, it costs three times that. Why?

== Office | contract: CONTRACT; price $600; stamp APPROVED; "Only one supplier approved"
Dr. Lee (point up, worried): The contract says we must buy from one company.
Mara (rubbing hands, happy): That’s how we get the lowest price, right?

== Shop | tender: TENDER; "Must be delivered in 48 hours"; "Only one brand allowed"; "Must use special vial"
Dana (explain, sly): The tender says: deliver fast. Only one brand. Special vial.
Ben Carter (showing paper, sneaky) [bubble]: We have the only special vials.

== Board | compare: Generic medicine $200 vs Special vial medicine $600
Faye (point, surprised): That’s three times more. And the medicine is exactly the same.
Dana (think, worried): The same dosage. Same ingredients. Just a different package.

== Office | flow: Hospital budget -> Ben Carter $600; Ben Carter -> Pharmacy $150; Ben Carter -> Mara 30% kickback
Dana (explain, sly): The hospital pays $600.
Dana: Ben Carter keeps $150 for the pharmacy. And gives Mara a 30% kickback.

== Home | bag: "Medicine for 200 patients"
Mara (smiles, happy) [bubble]: We’re all set. The medicine is safe and ready.
Faye (facepalm, sad): But it costs $600 per bottle. Not $200.

== Board | percent: 30% kickback to Mara
Dana (point, angry): That’s 30% of the extra cost — going straight to her pocket.
