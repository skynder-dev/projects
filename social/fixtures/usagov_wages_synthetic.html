Synthetic unpaid wages help

Who may be owed wages

You may claim only if the Wage and Hour Division recovered wages from your employer and the agency identifies you as an eligible worker.

What help exists

The search may show recovered wages; a search result does not guarantee payment.

How to apply

Search the official recovered-wages service and follow the agency's instructions.

Conditions and exceptions

You must prove your identity. This process does not cover wages the agency has not recovered. Other wage disputes require a different process.
