Synthetic help with energy bills

This synthetic page describes three separate forms of conditional energy assistance.

Find out if you are eligible for LIHEAP and apply

The synthetic LIHEAP example may help with household energy bills only if the responsible office determines that the household meets its requirements. A bill does not establish eligibility, and an application does not guarantee that assistance will be available. The office must review the household's circumstances, income documents and the costs for which it is requesting assistance. Do not treat a preliminary conversation or a search result as an approval. This example concerns bill assistance, not weatherization work or a decision to reconnect a utility.

In this synthetic example, you must ask the responsible LIHEAP office which documents it requires before submitting an application. The office may request additional information, and it may refuse an application that does not meet the applicable conditions. Keep the explanation of these restrictions with the description of help. A different household's application does not establish what this office will decide about your household. No payment amount, national income limit or application deadline is established by this synthetic page. The complete conditions must be read together with the instructions below.

  • Ask about LIHEAP eligibility. Contact the responsible office. You must meet its income and household requirements; a referral is not an approval.
  • Apply to the LIHEAP office. Follow that office's application instructions and supply the documents it requests. Do not assume that every energy expense is covered.
  • Keep the LIHEAP exception. This synthetic bill-help example does not decide whether a disconnected utility will be restored.

LIHEAP documents

You must provide the identity and income evidence required by the responsible LIHEAP office. Only that office can decide eligibility.

Find out what to do if your utilities were disconnected

The synthetic disconnected-utilities example describes where to ask for help only if the responsible office can consider the household's circumstances. A disconnection notice does not establish eligibility, and an application does not guarantee that service will be restored. The office must review the household's circumstances, account documents and the service for which it is requesting assistance. Do not treat a preliminary conversation or a search result as an approval. This example concerns a disconnected utility, not weatherization work or automatic payment of an energy bill.

In this synthetic example, you must ask the responsible disconnection-help office which documents it requires before submitting an application. The office may request additional information, and it may refuse an application that does not meet the applicable conditions. Keep the explanation of these restrictions with the description of help. A different household's application does not establish what this office will decide about your household. No payment amount, national income limit or application deadline is established by this synthetic page. The complete conditions must be read together with the instructions below.

  • Ask about disconnected utilities. Contact the responsible office and explain the notice. You must meet its requirements; a referral is not an approval.
  • Apply for disconnection help. Follow that office's application instructions and supply the documents it requests. Service restoration is not guaranteed.
  • Keep the disconnection exception. This synthetic example does not decide whether weatherization work is available.

Documents for disconnected utilities

You must provide the account and identity evidence required by the responsible office. Only that office can decide what help is available.

Page-wide state rules

Rules differ by state. For every program on this synthetic page, only the responsible office can decide eligibility. Help is not guaranteed.
