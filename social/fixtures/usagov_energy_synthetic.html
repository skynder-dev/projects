Synthetic energy bill help

This synthetic example describes conditional help with household energy bills.

Who may qualify

Households may qualify only if their income meets the local program's limits. In this synthetic example the limit is $25,000; it is not a national limit.

What you may receive

LIHEAP may help with energy bills. Help is not guaranteed and funds may be limited.

How to apply

Contact your state or tribal energy assistance office to ask how to apply.

Conditions and state rules

You must meet the applicable requirements. Rules differ by state. Only the responsible office can decide your eligibility.

Some expenses are excluded unless the local program allows them. Keep this exception with the help description.
