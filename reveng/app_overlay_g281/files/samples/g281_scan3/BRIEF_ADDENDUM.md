## What 72 killed ideas in scans 1-2 teach (do not repeat these mistakes; verify early, kill early)
1. A free official portal, free tool, open-source project or $0-$30/month incumbent already covers most wedges. Search for them FIRST (before building the story).
2. Regulatory or licence "moats" usually protect incumbents or are open to anyone who takes a course; a stranger has no track record, no existing relationships and no existing audience.
3. Data panels, marketplaces and communities need a pre-existing audience or supply; "we will build the audience" is not an edge.
4. Success-fee / contingency / flat-price offers are already sold; a lower price is not an edge.
5. One-time purchases give no repeat revenue; prefer recurring obligations or repeat usage that exist TODAY.
6. Check that the entrant can reach the first 10 paying customers without an audience: name the concrete channel and cite evidence it works for strangers.
Aim for ideas where a structural edge is *obtainable by a stranger within 365 days* (for example an acquirable existing asset, a platform channel that demonstrably sends buyers to newcomers, exclusive access you can lawfully obtain, or unit economics that are structurally better and shown with sourced numbers). If you honestly find none in a direction, kill it and move on; do not pad.
